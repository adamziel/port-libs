Remote Work Policy Memo

This internal memo describes the default expectations for teams that split work between office and remote locations.

Summary

Managers should keep the policy simple enough for repeat use while documenting exceptions that affect payroll, security, or customer commitments.

  • Publish team availability windows.
  • Keep customer-facing escalation paths current.
  • Review equipment and access requests quarterly.

Approval levels

Checklist

  1. Confirm the employee has a secure workstation.
  2. Record the expected working region.
  3. Link the schedule in the team handbook.
